# 🌿 Herbalism & Spiritual Healing

A digital sanctuary exploring the intersection of physical plant medicine, clinical herbalism, and ancient spiritual healing traditions.

## 📖 About The Project

This website was created to bridge the gap between the scientific and the sacred. It serves as a comprehensive resource for those seeking to nurture their body with plant allies while tending to the energetic and spiritual aspects of their well-being.

The design aesthetic is intentionally "Modern Classic" — grounded, earthy, and timeless — ensuring the digital experience feels as calming as the practice itself.

## ✨ Features

*   **Services:** Detailed breakdown of holistic consultations, energy work, and custom herbal formulations.
*   **About:** The personal journey and philosophy behind the practice.
*   **Testimonials:** Real experiences from clients who have walked the healing path.
*   **The Journal (Blog):** Articles on herbal energetics, ethical foraging, and seasonal rituals.
*   **Contact:** A direct way for potential clients to reach out.
*   **Mobile-First Design:** Built to look beautiful and function perfectly on any device.

## 🛠️ Built With

This project is built using clean, accessible web standards to ensure it remains fast and functional for years to come:

*   **HTML5** - Semantic structure
*   **CSS3** - Custom styling, responsive layout, and modern typography
*   **Google Fonts** - *Cormorant Garamond* (Headings) & *Inter* (Body)
*   **GitHub Pages** - Hosting and deployment
